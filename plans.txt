DIGITAL AUDIO SOUND ENGINEERS RECRUITER SUBSYSTEM.

Subsystem Codename: DASE MARKET PLACE.
DASE is an acronym for Digital Audio Sound Engineers.

SIGN UP AS DASE MARKET PLACE PROFESSIONAL _-_ this is name/tag for a clickable button that allows user to register as a sound engineer expert.
SIGN UP AS DASE MARKET PLACE CLIENT _-_ this is name/tag for a clickable button that allows potential customers register to use the system.
BROWSE DASE MARKET PLACE DATABASE _-_ this is name/tag for a clickable button that allows visitors/users to browse database of registered sound engineers, read their profiles, listen to their production samples and connect directly.
SEARCH & CONNECT _-_ this is name/tag for a “Search Bar/Field” with a clickable button which allows user to search and connect with any registered sound engineer, using their given names or business names to find them.

**Upload Production Samples _-_ this shall be a clickable action button on the Contractor’s dashboard to allow users upload audio files onto the system from their devices.

SYSTEM PERFORMANCE AND ACTIVITIES.
_Account owner shall be able to upload up to ten production samples “maximum” in both MP3 and WAV formats.
_Account owner shall be able to write caption and description for each production samples uploaded.
_Account owner shall be able to [Add Face To The Voice] by uploading photo/graphics related to individual production uploaded.
_Registered users shall be able to write reviews on production samples uploaded.
_Registered users shall be able to Approve or Disapprove production samples uploaded.
_Account owner shall be able to reply reviews and comments posted by other users.
_Other registered users shall be able to reply, approve and disapprove the reviews/comments by other users.
_Account owner shall be able to remove and replace production samples from time to time.

*CHATROOM SYSTEM DOCUMENTATION 
_CHAT NOW _-_ this shall be a clickable action button that is found right inside the homepages of both the registered Contractor and Clients respectively. This “Chat Now” button when clicked allows both parties to connect and talk business via text messaging.

*INVOICE SYSTEM DOCUMENTATION 
_PREPARE AND SEND INVOICES _-_ this shall be a clickable action button right on the dashboard of the Contractor. It is a folder for the invoices ever prepared. It’s also a place to send invoices to clients, using their details as registered on DASE MARKET PLACE. If invoice folder is empty, the system shows nothing to view.

*SERVICE RATING FEATURE 
RATE THIS DASE MARKET PLACE CONTRACTOR _-_ this shall be a clickable “frontend” action button with timestamps feature to rate the services of a given Contractor on the DASE MARKET PLACE platform from 1 to 5 stars.

*STATUS UPDATES FEATURE 
There shall be a status update feature with timestamps on every account created, where DASE MARKET PLACE Contractors can advertise their services, announce promotions and discounts, plus more related. Users using the feature are able to upload short videos, short audio files or photos, or text per post, and other users are able to view Status Updates, Approve or Disapprove posts, comment on and reply Status Update posts.

*SENDING AUDIO FILES TO CLIENTS 
_UPLOAD FINISHED PRODUCTION _-_ this shall be a clickable button on Contractor’s dashboard. When clicked, the system ask the user to upload files from their device. After upload is complete, user shall be able to copy and share the download link with the Client that paid for the audio production by pasting the URL onto the chatroom they both initiated the production deal and that’s how Client gets the finished production.
